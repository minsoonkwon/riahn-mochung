About

In Mochung-dong near Seowon University,
this is RIAHN Hair Mochung Seowondae.

RIAHN Hair Mochung Seowondae is a well-loved hair salon for clients in Cheongju, Seowon-gu, Mochung-dong and around Seowon University. Popular styles for women include short cuts, bobs, tassel cuts, layered cuts, hush cuts and sleek cuts, along with vintage, hippie, hug, layered, build and bob perms, plus color and bleach. Men love our dandy cuts, leaf cuts, Gail perms, parted perms, shadow perms and root perms, along with milk brown and ash brown color.

From your first visit to every visit after, we create a style made just for you in a relaxed setting.

You'll find us on the 1st floor of 44 Seowonnam-ro, Seowon-gu, Cheongju. Turn right at the salon and the free parking lot is right there. Pets are welcome.

Why RIAHN Hair Mochung Seowondae
