How much is root color?
Root color is ₩40,000 for gray coverage, ₩50,000 for fashion color, ₩50,000 for premium gray coverage and ₩60,000 for premium fashion color.
How much is full color?
Full color is ₩55,000 for gray coverage, ₩65,000 for fashion color, ₩65,000 for premium gray coverage and ₩75,000 for premium fashion color.

Yes. A basic root bleach is ₩60,000, a basic bleach ₩90,000 (1 session), a design bleach ₩130,000 (1 session) and black color removal ₩150,000 (1 session).
