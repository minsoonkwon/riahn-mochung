From vintage and build perms
to volume straightening and repair perms

RIAHN Hair Mochung Seowondae specializes in a wide range of perm styles. A basic perm is ₩60,000 for healthy hair and ₩70,000 for damaged hair, a design or specialty perm ₩80,000, and volume straightening ₩120,000 for healthy hair and ₩140,000 for damaged hair. Perm and straightening prices depend on your hair condition (healthy or damaged).

Deputy Director Miji Lee specializes in build perms, CS perms, iron perms, leaf perms, cowlick correction and repair perms, and Director Yeongju in build perms, leaf perms, iron perms and CS curls. Gail perms and down perms are popular with men. For care to go with your perm, see Treatments.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who wants a new mood with a vintage, build or leaf perm
  • Anyone who wants a smooth, calm look with volume straightening or straightening + setting
  • Anyone who needs cowlick correction or a repair perm for damaged hair
  • Men looking for a down perm or Gail perm

FAQ

Perm FAQ

How much is a basic perm?
A basic perm is ₩60,000 for healthy hair and ₩70,000 for damaged hair. A design or specialty perm is ₩80,000.
How much are volume straightening and repair perms?
Volume straightening is ₩120,000 for healthy hair and ₩140,000 for damaged hair, and a repair perm (damaged hair) is ₩200,000.
Do you offer men's perms?
Yes. A down perm (cut included) is ₩35,000, a full down perm (cut included) ₩50,000, a men's basic perm ₩60,000, a men's design perm ₩70,000, and men's volume straightening or iron perms ₩100,000 for healthy hair and ₩120,000 for damaged hair.
