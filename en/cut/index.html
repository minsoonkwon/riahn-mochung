haircuts in Mochung-dong

At RIAHN Hair Mochung Seowondae, a women's basic cut is ₩18,000, a women's design cut ₩20,000, a men's basic cut ₩15,000 and a men's design cut ₩18,000. Cuts for elementary to high school students and kids aged 7 and under start from ₩15,000.

Short cuts and bob cuts are popular with women, and dandy cuts with men. If you'd like volume too, see Perms & Straightening.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who wants a new look with a short cut or bob
  • Men looking for a dandy cut
  • Students from elementary to high school, and kids aged 7 and under
  • Anyone who'd like a scalp scaling with their cut

Haircut FAQ

How much is a women's haircut?
A women's basic cut is ₩18,000, a women's design cut ₩20,000 and a women's scalp scaling cut ₩28,000. A bangs trim is ₩5,000.
How much is a men's haircut?
A men's basic cut is ₩15,000, a men's design cut ₩18,000 and a men's scalp scaling cut ₩25,000.
Are there prices for students and kids?
Yes. Girls' cuts for elementary to high school students are ₩15,000, boys' basic cuts ₩15,000 and boys' design cuts ₩16,000, and cuts for kids aged 7 and under are ₩15,000.
