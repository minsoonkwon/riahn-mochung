FAQ

FAQ

Answers to common questions about booking, parking, hours and prices.

Home/FAQ
How do I book?
You can book your preferred date and time in real time on Naver Booking. Phone booking (+82-43-283-0663) is also available, and you can message us on Instagram.
Is parking available?
Yes, parking is free. Turn right at the salon and the parking lot is right there.
What are your opening hours?
We're open 10:00–19:30 on weekdays, Saturdays and Sundays. Check Naver Booking for the last booking time and closed days.
When are you closed?
Check Naver Booking for the last booking time and closed days.
How much are your services?
A women's basic cut is ₩18,000, a men's basic cut ₩15,000, a basic perm from ₩60,000 (healthy hair), root color from ₩40,000, full color from ₩55,000 and a basic treatment ₩35,000. See each service page for full prices.
Are there prices for students and kids?
Yes. Students' cuts (elementary to high school) start from ₩15,000 (boys' design cut ₩16,000), and cuts for kids aged 7 and under are ₩15,000.
Is a shampoo included with a haircut?
Haircut prices do not include a shampoo; a shampoo is +₩5,000. If you add a haircut to your color, the cut is +₩10,000.
Can I book a specific designer?
Please choose your preferred designer when you book. You can see each designer's specialties on the Our Designers page.
Do you offer scalp care or hair loss care?
Yes. We offer a range of extra services such as head spa, scalp treatments and hair loss care. Scalp care starts from ₩60,000 per session.
Can I bring my pet?
Yes, pets are welcome.

Didn't find your answer? Call us during opening hours (10:00–19:30).
